What ESTABLISHED means here, and what it does not. For this object it means one thing only: the object is authentic and unaltered — its signature is valid, its evidence hashes match, and its identity re-derives from content. It does not mean QPF still stands behind the offer written inside it. That offer was the $2 / $5 USD “pay after ESTABLISHED, FAILED = $0” price, and it is withdrawn. It made revenue depend on the verdict, which is a fee for good verdicts. The object's bytes are untouched so its identity still reproduces; the offer inside is dead.

The gist ships a pinned copy of the verifier as of its publish date (sha256 9439e09d…), which still verifies this bundle. The current verifier in the repo is a later copy that also understands the v2 policy. The gist description has been updated to cite the matching content id (ebda8d38…).
