What the results mean

ESTABLISHED
The available evidence supports the specific claim.
NOT ESTABLISHED
The evidence does not support the claim.
UNKNOWN
The available evidence cannot settle it.

Verification is not authorization

QPF records what the evidence establishes. It does not turn a verification result into authorization, a security guarantee, or an investment recommendation.
