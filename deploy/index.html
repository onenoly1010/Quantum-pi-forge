AI doesn’t need more blind trust. It needs verifiable boundaries.

Quantum Pi Forge (QPF) and the OINIO Soul System are built around a simple principle:

A claim is not proof. Proof is not permission.

Intended verification model

Within QPF’s intended verification model, evidence leads to explicitly scoped outcomes:

  • ESTABLISHED Supported within the defined scope.
  • NOT ESTABLISHED The evidence does not establish the claim.
  • UNKNOWN The available evidence is insufficient to reach a conclusion.

A verification receipt cannot establish more than its evidence supports. A verdict does not grant permission to act. Execution requires separate authorization.

The goal is not to ask people to trust another AI system. It is to make the evidence, verification process, and limits of each conclusion open to inspection.

Current status: QPF is preserved as a technical record, not represented as an open public verification service. The design describes intended boundaries, not a universal AI standard or a claim that every operational property has been independently verified.

Less blind trust. More visible evidence. Clearer boundaries. Human authority preserved.
