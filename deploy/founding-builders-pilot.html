Not a launch. Not a token campaign. A small pilot to learn whether a private AI operator actually saves builders time on real work.

Who this is for

  • 0G developers — AI infra, agents, compute, data, verification-minded builders
  • Pi ecosystem builders — app developers and utility creators (not “everyone on Pi”)
  • Local AI / open-source developers — Ollama, coding assistants, MCP, agent tooling

If you already have a painful workflow this week, you’re a better fit than someone who only wants a product tour.

What you get

  • A guided 15–30 minute path: private AI operator under your control
  • One real task from your work (docs, verify, understand a flow, automate a step)
  • Direct feedback loop with the maintainer
  • Influence: repeated pains become what we improve next
  • No wallet, no mint, no investment pitch

What we’re asking for

  • Try one workflow
  • Tell us what happened (time, friction, would you use it again?)
  • If it doesn’t help — tell us why

Honest “this didn’t fit” is as valuable as “I’ll use this again.”

Why spend 15 minutes?

You leave with a clear answer: did this save time on a task you already have — or not? No pitch deck. No architecture lecture required.

Inspect anytime: public repo, verification portal, deployed status (economics locked by design).

Not economic activation. Public mint, liquidity, and protocol revenue remain not authorized. This pilot is utility and feedback only.
