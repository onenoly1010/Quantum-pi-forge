How do I create an evidence package for an AI-generated result?

AI output is not evidence. Re-checkable methods are.

1. The problem

Models produce confident prose about deploys, agents, and status. Partners and you still need: timestamped methods, raw results, and clear separation of “model said” vs “chain/RPC returned.”

2. What a good package contains

  • The claim (one sentence)
  • Method used (RPC, explorer, script name)
  • Result (raw or summarized without spin)
  • Confidence + unknowns
  • What was not checked

QPF’s posture: prepare with AI if you want; authority and verification stay human-readable and re-runnable.

3. Example shape

Certificate #000 style: verified chain and code presence; mint/LP explicitly gated; revenue and adoption not verified.
