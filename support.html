Public infrastructure

Support the Forge

Quantum Pi Forge is human-led, AI-supported public infrastructure for sovereign AI.
It runs because real people choose to sustain it.

Your contribution keeps:

  • Verification public and free to use
  • Documentation open and maintained
  • Local AI tools free to download and run
  • Governance independent of any funder
  • Infrastructure running for the next person who finds this

How support is used

  • Node infrastructure and hosting costs
  • Independent audit fees
  • Documentation and onboarding work
  • Community builder support
  • Open-source tooling maintenance

All treasury activity is on-chain and publicly verifiable. No funds move without human approval. AI never holds keys.

Ways to help

  • Direct patronage or builder sponsorship
  • Grant or bounty opportunities
  • Technical review and independent verification
  • Plain-language feedback and documentation
  • Community onboarding and outreach
  • Infrastructure or hardware support

Human custody rule

AI supports visibility, proposals, receipts, and explanation only. AI does not hold private keys, sign transactions, approve spending, or move funds. Any financial action must be human-reviewed and transparent.

What we will never ask for

  • Seed phrases or private keys
  • Blind approvals or unsigned transactions
  • Custody over anyone else's funds

This page exists so support can move from abstract documentation into real human and community benefit. The forge is a public good. Sustaining it is voluntary — and meaningful.
