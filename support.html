Support / Build With Quantum Pi Forge

Quantum Pi Forge is a human-led, AI-supported verification and community-building system. The goal is to make technical systems understandable, transparent, and useful for real people.

What support helps with

  • keeping the human builder stable
  • maintaining public documentation and verification
  • supporting builders, testers, and community onboarding
  • turning verified systems into usable public tools
  • reducing overcomplexity so everyday people can participate

Human custody rule

AI supports visibility, proposals, receipts, and explanation only. AI does not hold private keys, sign transactions, approve spending, or move funds.

Ways to help

  • builder sponsorship
  • grant or bounty opportunities
  • technical review
  • plain-language feedback
  • community onboarding support
  • infrastructure or hardware support

Current boundary

QPF does not ask for seed phrases, private keys, blind approvals, or custody over anyone else's funds. Any financial action must be human-reviewed and transparent.

This page exists so support can move from abstract documentation into real human and community benefit.
