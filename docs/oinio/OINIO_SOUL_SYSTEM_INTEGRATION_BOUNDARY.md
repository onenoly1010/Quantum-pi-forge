Quantum Pi Forge remains the evidence and verification layer.

Core invariant:

Authenticity != Authority

## Boundaries

- No custody transfer is implied.
- No wallet authority is delegated to an agent.
- No automated execution is treated as human authorization.
- No observation receipt becomes a transfer instruction.
- No reviewer, funder, platform, or institution becomes project authority by interest alone.
