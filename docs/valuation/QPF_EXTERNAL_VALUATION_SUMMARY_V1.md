Quantum Pi Forge (QPF) and the OINIO Soul System are **early-stage sovereign AI and decentralized execution infrastructure**. Current value should be assessed through **replacement cost**, **audit-readiness**, **strategic utility**, **IP value**, and **future network potential** — not traditional earnings metrics.

QPF does **not** claim a finalized external fair-market valuation. Instead, it provides a **defensible evidence stack** for future appraisal, audit, grant review, licensing discussions, or strategic partnership diligence.

**Increase proof, not hype.**

## What QPF is

QPF combines:

- **Local AI observer** and sovereign operations posture
- **Blockchain evidence** and deterministic verification concepts
- **Human governance gates** for mint, liquidity, staking, bridge, and Guardian authority
- **Public trust surfaces** (onboarding, deployed addresses, safety documentation)

Reviewers should treat QPF as **infrastructure under disciplined activation control**, not as a launched revenue business or token-first product.
