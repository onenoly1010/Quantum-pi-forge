### Rules

1. Prefer the **defensible project description** for abstracts and openers.
2. Claims labeled **Verified** may be stated as present capability with proof path cited.
3. Claims labeled **Implemented but gated** must say gated/disabled/not economically active.
4. Claims labeled **Experimental** must say experimental/local/lab.
5. Claims labeled **Planned** may appear only as roadmap, never as live.
6. Forbidden phrases from the status table are not allowed in this kit or derived emails/decks.
7. Do not invent block numbers, user counts, trust scores, or “first in industry” claims.
8. On-chain addresses must not be presented as canonically verified while `contracts/DEPLOYED_ADDRESSES.md` remains **Pending**.
