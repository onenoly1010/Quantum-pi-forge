# QPF Fair Market Value Baseline Memo v1

Status: INTERNAL_BASELINE_NOT_EXTERNAL_APPRAISAL
Created: 2026-07-04T05:09:05.937Z

## Purpose
This memo frames Quantum Pi Forge and the OINIO Soul System as early-stage sovereign AI and decentralized execution infrastructure. It separates proven facts, internal replacement-cost estimates, strategic option value, and evidence required before any external fair-market valuation claim.

## Current valuation posture
QPF is not currently valued as a mature revenue business. The strongest present method is replacement cost plus strategic option value. External FMV remains unproven until supported by third-party audit, usage metrics, revenue, licensing demand, grants, or formal partner interest.

## Conservative valuation bands
- Internal replacement-cost floor: 750k to 2M USD equivalent.
- Audit/security-adjusted infrastructure value: 1M to 3M USD equivalent, conditional on independent review.
- Strategic partnership/licensing range: 3M to 10M USD equivalent, conditional on buyer need, adoption, and proof-of-use.
- Network value: variable and not claimed until measurable usage exists.

## Non-transferable sovereignty note
Code, documentation, receipts, deployment patterns, and tooling may be valued or licensed. Guardian authority, sovereign legitimacy, and human-rooted execution truth are not cleanly transferable without changing the system design.
