# Verification Policy

## Required evidence

Every production-relevant change must identify:

1. Source commit or pull request.
2. Targeted local validation.
3. Required CI status.
4. Deployment result, when deployment is authorized.
5. Public verification result, when a public surface changes.

## 0G verification

Use 0gskills for implementation guidance, but independently verify
production-critical facts against `https://docs.0g.ai/`. Record the consulted
URLs or version identifiers in the relevant deployment or verification report.

Disagreements between sources are blockers, not choices for the AI to resolve.

## Commercial claims

Do not claim traffic, customer demand, paid engagements, funding, partnerships,
or third-party verification without independent supporting evidence. Unknown
metrics remain `not tracked`.

## Evidence locations

Use the relevant pull request, issue, receipt, or document under
`docs/activation/command/revenue/` so another reviewer can reproduce the
decision context.
