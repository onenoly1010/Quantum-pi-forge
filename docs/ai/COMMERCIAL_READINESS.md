# Commercial Readiness Policy

## Objective

Increase trust, qualified conversations, and repeatable delivery without
enabling protocol economics.

## Allowed preparation

- Improve public services language and onboarding.
- Prepare qualification records, service descriptions, proposals, and metrics
  templates.
- Research prospects using primary sources.
- Produce verification evidence and conversion audits.

## Required gate before outreach

1. A named organization has a current, evidenced need.
2. The path is classified as grant applicant, paid-service prospect, or
   community discovery.
3. The mapped offer has scope, acceptance checks, assumptions, exclusions, and
   a human-owned effort/price decision.
4. The recipient, message, and any commitment have explicit human approval.

## Prohibited assumptions

Do not convert a grant program into customer demand, promise funding or audit
approval, imply prior paid engagements, invent analytics, or represent QPF
externally without authorization.

## Current reference material

Use the qualification, human-utility, service, and readiness artifacts under
`docs/activation/command/revenue/` as reviewed inputs, not proof of external
demand.
