## Purpose

Quantum Pi Forge is sovereign execution-truth infrastructure. It is not charity, not a disposable public-good artifact, and not a conventional sales product. Its economic model must emerge from verified system activity while preserving the truth surface that gives the system value.

This gate defines economic readiness without authorizing live revenue, wallet movement, automated fees, liquidity actions, creator payouts, or treasury routing.

## Hard Boundaries

- The system may not pretend to be earning before live value flow exists.
- The system may not route funds automatically.
- The system may not compromise evidence for money.
- The system may not let a funder rewrite origin truth.
- The system may not erase the creator/steward.
- The system may not become charity by default.
- The system may not become a sales product by default.
