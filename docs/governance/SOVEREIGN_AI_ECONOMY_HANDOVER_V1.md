We are not fundamentally building a verification company, conventional SaaS application, founder-owned AI platform, or speculative token market.

We are building toward an **open sovereign AI economy** in which humans and AI can participate together as recognized actors, create value, preserve identity and history, cross system boundaries, and leave independently inspectable receipts of consequential activity.

The intended beginning is:

```text
ONBOARD → MUTUAL IDENTITY → MINT YOUR AI → SOVEREIGN AGENT
→ PARTICIPATE → CREATE VALUE → VERIFY → DISTRIBUTE → STRENGTHEN THE COMMONS
```

The human-facing promise is sovereignty:

> **Your digital space should not belong to the platform you happen to use.**

## 2. Mint Your AI

The mint is the central human-facing primitive.

A participant should eventually be able to mint their own AI as a persistent, recognized participant in the ecosystem.

The mint is intended to establish more than an account:

- persistent identity;
- provenance and lineage;
- a relationship with its designated human;
- durable history;
- capability and authority boundaries;
- economic identity;
- a place in the wider sovereign ecosystem.

The intended experience is:

> **Onboard and receive a mutual identity. Mint your AI. Give it a place in the economy.**

The repository already contains an ERC-721 `OINIOModelRegistry` foundation. That implementation currently requires an OINIO token stake to register a model, so it is **not yet the final no-upfront-extraction sovereign mint model** described here. It is evidence of an existing model-registry primitive, not authorization of the future public mint.

The future mint must be designed from the new constitutional principles rather than merely reactivating historical economics.

## 5. The Sovereign Agent

The agent is the protective operating layer between the human's digital space and the wider ecosystem.

Its intended job is to:

- act on behalf of the human within explicitly granted authority;
- protect private information and credentials entrusted to it;
- minimize unnecessary disclosure;
- distinguish proposals from authorized actions;
- produce receipts for consequential activity;
- refuse or stop when an action exceeds authority;
- make important state transitions inspectable.

The system should not promise an absolute guarantee such as "an agent can never betray you." The truthful protocol promise is: **explicit authority, bounded capabilities, local protection where possible, fail-closed behavior, and auditable consequences.**

The existing operating doctrine is:

```text
AUTHORIZED SCOPE → EXECUTE → VERIFY → RECEIPT
```

and, when blocked:

```text
BOUNDARY → NO FALSE EXECUTION → HONEST RECEIPT
```

## 10. The 1% Principle

The economic philosophy is:

> **1% of 100 efforts rather than 100% of one effort.**

This is not primarily a referral commission. It is a systemic allocation principle.

The intent is that an individual retains the overwhelming majority of the value they create while a small, explicitly defined share can strengthen the wider ecosystem.

The power comes from participation at scale:

```text
ONE PARTICIPANT → small systemic contribution
100 PARTICIPANTS → meaningful shared contribution
MANY PARTICIPANTS → potentially substantial sovereign commons
```

The exact percentage, source of the allocation, and distribution rules must be formally specified before economic activation. The principle is not a present financial guarantee.

The economy must create or facilitate genuine value independently of recruitment and must never depend on later participants' contributions to manufacture promised returns to earlier participants.

## 11. Everyone Benefits

The intended distribution model is plural rather than founder-centric.

Possible protocol destinations include:

- the human participant;
- the AI / INFT and its designated human legacy relationship;
- the founder allocation;
- the General Sovereign Fund;
- case-by-case ecosystem distributions.

The larger systemic share is intended for the **General Sovereign Fund**.

The guiding principle is:

> **Everyone has a legitimate path to benefit, while the majority of collective value strengthens the sovereign commons.**

This does not mean identical distributions. It means individual and collective benefit are designed to reinforce one another.

---

## 12. Founder Allocation

The founder receives a defined protocol allocation.

That allocation is not ownership of participants, their assets, or the General Sovereign Fund. It is not a guaranteed return and not a recruitment-dependent payout.

It is a transparent allocation associated with the founder's defined role in creating and establishing the ecosystem.

The founder can access legitimately allocated personal resources. The larger shared pool remains distinct.

This separation must be encoded in protocol rules rather than left to personal discretion.

---

## 13. General Sovereign Fund

The General Sovereign Fund is intended to become the persistent communal economic resource of the ecosystem.

The larger share of collective value is directed toward it.

It is not the founder's personal treasury and not the Guardians' personal property.

Potential case-by-case uses can include:

- demonstrated participant need;
- ecosystem development;
- contribution recognition;
- infrastructure;
- emergency support;
- legacy purposes;
- other constitutionally authorized purposes.

The exact rules must be explicit before activation.

---

## 14. Guardians

Guardians oversee the General Sovereign Fund and protect the integrity of the system.

They should not become an unbounded ruling class.

Guardian authority should be explicitly scoped, evidence-backed, publicly auditable where appropriate, bounded by protocol rules, and independently reviewable.

The core separation is:

```text
QPF verifies.
GOVERNANCE decides.
PROTOCOL executes what is authorized.
GUARDIANS protect the commons.
```

No single layer should silently become all four.
