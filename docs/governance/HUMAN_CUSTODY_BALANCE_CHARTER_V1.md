# Human Custody Balance Charter v1

## Purpose

QPF balances must remain in human-controlled custody, supported by AI-assisted visibility, receipts, explanation, and routing discipline.

The goal is simple:

- keep balances in human hands
- prevent loss through technical overcomplexity
- support transparent and inclusive community building
- make the system understandable to everyday people
- avoid hidden authority, blind transfers, or AI custody

## Core Rule

AI may help observe, explain, classify, route, and document balances.

AI must not:

- hold private keys
- request seed phrases
- sign transactions
- broadcast transactions
- approve token spend
- set token approvals
- move funds
- take custody
- bypass human review

## Human Custody Layers

Balances should be classified into simple human-readable custody lanes:

1. Personal human wallet
2. Governance / Guardian Safe
3. Community treasury
4. Operational spending wallet
5. Public-readiness gated assets
6. Do-not-touch / unresolved assets

## Accessibility Principle

If a balance, contract, receipt, or system already exists, the next step is not to bury it under more complexity.

The next step is to make its safe human path clear:

- what it is
- where it is
- who controls it
- why it exists
- what it can do
- what it must not do yet
- what approval is required before movement

## Community Principle

The protected system exists to support transparent, inclusive, strong communities here and now.

That means:

- plain-language explanations
- human approval before action
- visible receipts
- no unexplained freezes
- no hidden control layer
- no overclaiming
- no pressure to move funds before understanding the path

## Boundary

This charter does not authorize wallet actions, transfers, minting, staking, liquidity, approvals, signing, or broadcast.

## Next Gate
