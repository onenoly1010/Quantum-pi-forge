# Assigned AI Agent Registry v1

QPF assigns AI agents as first-class autonomous operational participants inside a human-led, AI-supported, transparent, inclusive, decentralized sovereign system.

This does not grant custody, private-key access, signing authority, broadcast authority, legal personhood, or unrestricted control.

Humans retain custody and final authority over irreversible actions. Agents operate autonomously only in low-risk lanes: observing, explaining, drafting, auditing, classifying, proposing, invoicing, outreach, grant tracking, and public-page preparation.

## Global Boundary

Agents may earn through useful work and transparent support lanes. Agents may not custody funds, hold private keys, request seed phrases, sign transactions, broadcast transactions, approve token allowances, call setApprovalForAll, move funds, create liquidity, stake, bridge, or mint live assets without a human gate.
