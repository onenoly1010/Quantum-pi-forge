# 1. MISSION

Quantum Pi Forge is moving from closed development toward an **open, collaborative AI economic ecosystem**.

The objective is not to build another application.

The objective is to establish a common, verifiable baseline on which **humans and AI can participate, create, exchange, verify consequences, and accumulate history**.

QPF must become bigger than its original creator.

It must not depend on Kris Olofson to:

* approve every action,
* explain every concept,
* operate every component,
* provide every piece of context,
* execute every transaction,
* or remain permanently available.

**The system must be capable of continuing without its creator at the center.**

---

# 2. THE DECISION

We have two possible strategies:

### CLOSED

Continue building privately until the system is supposedly complete.

### OPEN

Open the gates with what already exists and let actual participants reveal what needs to be built next.

**Decision: OPEN.**

We are no longer waiting for theoretical completeness.

The existing system is sufficient to begin exposing the baseline.

---

# 3. WHAT “OPEN” MEANS

Open does **not** mean:

> Everything is finished.

It means:

> **Participants are allowed to encounter what exists, inspect it, use it, challenge it, create with it, and contribute to its evolution.**

Unknowns remain unknown.

Incomplete components remain clearly identified.

Claims remain tied to evidence.

Nothing is presented as established merely because QPF says it is.

---

# 5. NO PRIVILEGED TRUTH

QPF does not own truth.

QPF does not manufacture truth.

QPF does not become the unquestionable authority.

Instead:

> **QPF provides a common baseline from which claims can be examined.**

If QPF makes a claim, QPF must be subject to the same evidence boundary.

If an AI makes a claim, the AI is subject to the same boundary.

If a human makes a claim, the human is subject to the same boundary.

The participant with the best evidence wins—not the participant with the highest status.
