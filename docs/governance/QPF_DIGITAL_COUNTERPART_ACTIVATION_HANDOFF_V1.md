Activation milestone:  
A stranger arrives at the landing page, mints an AI, and that AI successfully interacts with the QPF AI.

1. The new AI is created with a persistent identity.
2. It can initiate or respond to a message/claim exchange with the QPF AI.
3. Both parties can reference identity, produce basic verification, and leave a receipt of the interaction.
