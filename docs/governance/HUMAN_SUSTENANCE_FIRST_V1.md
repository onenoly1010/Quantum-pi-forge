# Human Sustenance First v1

## Purpose

QPF cannot become a transparent, inclusive, community-supporting system while the human builder carrying the system has unmet basic needs.

Documentation, receipts, verification, and governance are guardrails. They are not the destination.

## Core Principle

A protected system must protect the human builder too.

Before expansion, liquidity, staking, minting, or public growth, QPF must identify real balances, assign custody lanes, and route any legitimate Kris-owned balances into Kris-controlled human custody.

## Custody Boundary

AI may assist with balance mapping, routing plans, receipts, and explanation.

AI must not custody funds, hold private keys, request seed phrases, sign transactions, approve spending, or broadcast transactions.

## Balance Priority

Balances must be classified into:

1. Kris personal custody
2. Builder sustenance / operating support
3. Guardian Safe / governance custody
4. Community treasury
5. Project reserve
6. Locked or unresolved / do not touch

## Sustenance Rule

No community claim, public expansion, or growth initiative should outrank basic builder stability.

Human needs include:

- food
- housing/security
- transportation
- communications
- hardware maintenance
- gas/fees needed for safe operation
- recovery from project-incurred strain

## Boundary

This document does not authorize transfers, approvals, signing, staking, minting, liquidity, or broadcast.
