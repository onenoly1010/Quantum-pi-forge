# QPF Revenue Activation Loop v1

**Status:** `NO_SEND_QUEUE_AWAITING_HUMAN_APPROVAL`  
**Objective:** Convert verified capability into independently evidenced market demand and revenue.  
**Scope:** Customer acquisition only. No wallet, payment, signing, transfer, or automated bulk outreach action is authorized.

## Operating loop

```text
Prepare -> Verify -> Authorize -> Execute -> Prove
```

| Stage | AI operations layer | Kris |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prepare | Research prospects; draft and tailor messages; prepare offer material | Set commercial direction |
| Verify | Confirm public contact path, technical signal, message accuracy, and safety boundary | Review priority and accuracy |
| Authorize | Record the exact target, channel, message reference, and intended scope | Approve each external delivery |
| Execute | Prepare delivery context and track the pending result | Deliver through the approved channel |
| Prove | Record independently evidenced delivery, reply, engagement, and payment events | Confirm evidence when required |

## Offer used in this queue

**48-72 hour evidence / grant-readiness walkthrough:** a claim-versus-evidence map, reproducible verification results, risk and gap list, and reviewer/funder next actions for an AI or blockchain infrastructure project.

The public Work With Us page lists broader capabilities. Outbound messaging in this queue uses the single walkthrough offer; price, booking link, and commercial terms remain human-set before any paid engagement.

## Approval gates

1. Recheck the listed public channel immediately before approval.
2. Approve one target, one channel, and one message at a time.
3. Record `SENT` only from independent delivery evidence.
4. Record a conversation, engagement, or revenue only from independent evidence.
5. Never automate bulk delivery or imply that a prospect needs, accepts, or has purchased QPF services.
