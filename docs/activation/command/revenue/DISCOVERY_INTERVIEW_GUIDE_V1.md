# Discovery Interview Guide v1

**Purpose:** Learn whether a named organization's real problem fits a bounded
QPF service. This is not a sales script and may not be used externally without
human approval.

## Opening

"Thank you for sharing context. This conversation is to understand your current
workflow and whether a small evidence-first engagement would be useful. No
scope, price, or commitment is being made in this conversation."

## Questions

### Current workflow

1. What are you trying to make easier for a reviewer, partner, funder, or new
   contributor to understand?
2. What artifacts currently explain what is deployed, verified, or planned?
3. Where do people lose time or confidence in the current process?

### Pain and trust

4. What claims are hardest for others to verify today?
5. What has a skeptical reviewer or partner asked that you could not answer
   quickly?
6. What evidence would make a decision easier?

### Verification and scope

7. Which sources may be reviewed: public repo, deployment URL, documents, or
   client-approved artifacts?
8. What must remain private or excluded?
9. What would a useful first milestone look like?

### Decision and budget process

10. Who evaluates technical work, who approves scope, and who approves spend?
11. Is there a defined budget process or funding deadline?
12. What would prevent a small scoped engagement from moving forward?

## Close

Summarize the stated problem, confirm no promise has been made, and ask whether
the organization wants a human-reviewed written scope. Do not send one without
explicit authorization.
