## Evidence boundary

The current qualified opportunities are ecosystem funding pathways, not verified
paying customers. Their official sources evidence program focus and entry paths;
they do not evidence a specific organization's intent to purchase QPF services.

## Decision rule

Do not turn a program-level signal into a paid-service claim. A paid proposal
requires a named organization, a public or directly supplied problem statement,
and a human-approved contact path.
