# First Engagement Offers

**Status:** Draft-only. Human approval is required before any quote, agreement,
or external use.

## Offer A - Evidence-readiness walkthrough

**Best for:** A team with technical claims, a repository, deployment, or public
documentation that needs a clearer proof story.

**Customer problem:** "A reviewer cannot quickly tell what is proven, what is
planned, and how to reproduce our claims."

**Smallest engagement:**

1. Review a bounded public or client-supplied artifact set.
2. Map claims to evidence.
3. Run or document reproducible checks where available.
4. Deliver a risk and next-action brief.

**Expected outcome:** The customer has one concise record of evidence, gaps, and
the next highest-confidence action.

**Not included:** Funding applications, legal opinions, private-key access,
financial transactions, or protocol activation.

## Offer B - Deployment transparency baseline

**Best for:** A team whose source, release, deployed endpoint, and onboarding
documentation do not yet form a coherent public story.

**Customer problem:** "People cannot tell which version is live or how to verify
it."

**Smallest engagement:**

1. Inventory public deployment and source references.
2. Identify conflicts or missing links.
3. Draft a reproducible deployment-verification path.
4. Deliver a public-safe transparency baseline.

**Expected outcome:** A reviewer can find the current deployment, understand its
relationship to source, and follow stated verification steps.

## Offer C - Sovereign-AI boundary review

**Best for:** A team that needs a plain-language explanation of a local or
private AI deployment.

**Customer problem:** "Stakeholders cannot see where data is processed or what
the system requires to operate safely."

**Smallest engagement:**

1. Document the stated runtime and data boundary.
2. Identify assumptions that require validation.
3. Produce a reproducible environment checklist.
4. Deliver a non-legal operational summary.

**Expected outcome:** Stakeholders receive a clear explanation of the system's
locality, assumptions, and verification limits.
