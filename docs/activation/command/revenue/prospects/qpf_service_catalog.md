# QPF Service Catalog

**Status:** Draft service language. Pricing, commitments, agreements, and
external use require human authorization.

## 1. Evidence-readiness walkthrough

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Who benefits? | Teams preparing a grant, partner review, audit discussion, or technical handoff. |
| What problem disappears? | Unclear claims and scattered proof make it hard for another person to assess what is real. |
| Why now? | Before a review or submission, missing evidence is cheaper to identify than after a credibility problem. |
| Measurable result | Claim-to-evidence map, reproducible check record, risk list, and next-action brief. |

## 2. Deployment transparency package

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Who benefits? | Teams whose public deployment, source, and documentation are difficult to reconcile. |
| What problem disappears? | Reviewers must guess which environment, release, or artifact is current. |
| Why now? | Partners and contributors need a dependable technical reference before they can evaluate or integrate. |
| Measurable result | A deployment report linking source, public endpoint, verification method, and stated limitations. |

## 3. Sovereign-AI boundary review

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Who benefits? | Teams considering local or private AI operations. |
| What problem disappears? | Stakeholders cannot tell which processing is local, what leaves the environment, or what operational assumptions exist. |
| Why now? | Privacy and operational boundaries should be understood before a rollout or stakeholder review. |
| Measurable result | A boundary map, configuration assumptions, and reproducible environment checklist. |

## 4. Developer onboarding and verification path

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Who benefits? | Open-source teams with credible technology but high contributor onboarding friction. |
| What problem disappears? | New contributors cannot identify a safe first verification task or understand how to validate a release. |
| Why now? | A short, verifiable path improves the chance that interested developers can become contributors. |
| Measurable result | A first-task guide, acceptance checklist, and proof links. |

## Service boundaries

QPF does not provide legal advice, custody, private-key handling, token issuance,
liquidity work, staking activation, governance control, or a promise of funding,
audit approval, or commercial outcome.
