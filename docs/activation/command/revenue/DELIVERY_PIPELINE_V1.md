# Delivery Pipeline v1

**Status:** Internal process. Progress beyond preparation requires explicit human
authorization.

| Stage | Inputs | Outputs | Approval gate | Success criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Inquiry | Human-received message or approved referral | Intake record with no sensitive data | None for recording | Need and source are captured accurately. |
| Qualification | Named organization, primary-source evidence, problem statement | Fit score and path classification | Human approval before external response | Grant, service, or community path is explicit. |
| Discovery | Human-approved conversation or supplied artifacts | Discovery notes and bounded problem | Human approval before any meeting | Scope inputs and exclusions are understood. |
| Scope | Discovery notes and chosen playbook | Draft deliverables, assumptions, acceptance checks | Human approval | First milestone is bounded and feasible. |
| Proposal | Approved scope and human-owned commercial terms | Draft proposal | Human approval before sending | No unsupported promise, price, or timeline. |
| Human approval | Draft proposal and recipient | Explicit send/commit decision | Required | Exact action and constraints are approved. |
| Execution | Approved scope and client inputs | Work artifacts and evidence record | Human approval for any scope change | Work stays inside scope and no restricted action occurs. |
| Verification | Work artifacts and evidence package | Findings and limitations | Human review before delivery | Claims map to evidence and exclusions are explicit. |
| Delivery | Reviewed findings | Delivery packet and receipt | Human approval before sending | Acceptance criteria are met or exceptions recorded. |
| Closeout | Delivery packet and client decision | Closeout report and lessons | Human approval for follow-up | No unrecorded commitment remains. |

## Non-negotiable controls

- No autonomous outreach, proposal delivery, agreement, or financial action.
- No secret collection or handling in repository artifacts.
- No protocol, wallet, governance, or contract action is part of service delivery.
- Unknowns are recorded as unknowns, not converted into customer claims.
