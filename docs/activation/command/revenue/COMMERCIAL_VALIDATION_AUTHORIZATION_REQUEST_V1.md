# Commercial Validation Authorization Request v1

**Status:** Draft for human decision. This document grants no authority and
cannot be used as an external message, proposal, or commitment.

## Objective

Test whether a named organization has a current, costly trust or
deployment-transparency problem that a small QPF evidence-readiness engagement
could help clarify.

## Commercial hypothesis

We believe that a small technical team preparing for review, funding, partnership,
or onboarding may have difficulty showing what is deployed, what can be verified,
and what remains uncertain. It may value a bounded evidence-readiness or
deployment-transparency baseline before pursuing more expensive audit, compliance,
or developer-relations work.

This is a hypothesis, not a claim of demand.

## Falsification criteria

Consider the hypothesis unsupported if, across the authorized sample:

1. no participant identifies a current, material workflow or trust problem;
2. participants already solve the problem satisfactorily with existing tools or
   vendors;
3. no participant identifies a decision owner, budget path, or meaningful
   consequence for leaving the problem unresolved; or
4. the useful work is primarily a formal audit, standards certification, or
   service outside QPF's documented capability.

## Permitted actions after explicit approval

- Send the human-approved introductory message to the approved named targets.
- Request a learning-focused discovery conversation.
- Use `DISCOVERY_INTERVIEW_GUIDE_V1.md` as an approved-question reference.
- Record contact date, approved message version, response status, and factual
  feedback in the approved evidence location.

## Forbidden actions

- Claim a partnership, customer relationship, adoption, endorsement, or funding.
- State or imply that QPF performs formal audits, certification, or compliance
  assessment.
- Quote prices, negotiate terms, send a proposal, sign an agreement, or make a
  financial commitment.
- Offer ownership, equity, tokens, wallet actions, or protocol/economic changes.
- Make technical guarantees or disclose non-public information.
- Expand the contact list, channel, message, or scope without new approval.

## Learning objectives

1. Is the stated problem current, material, and experienced by the target role?
2. Who owns the workflow and bears the consequence of inadequate evidence?
3. What workaround, vendor, process, or budget category exists today?
4. Which smallest outcome would be valuable enough to explore further?
5. Does the need fit QPF's documented evidence-readiness or
   deployment-transparency scope?
