# Competitive Review v1

**Purpose:** Position QPF honestly against documented alternatives. This is not
marketing copy.

| Alternative | What it does | Where QPF may complement it | Where QPF is weaker or unproven | Adoption risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| OpenSSF Scorecard (https://securityscorecards.dev/) | Automated open-source security checks. | QPF can organize broader claim, deployment, and documentation evidence around a project. | QPF is not a substitute for automated security scoring and has no equivalent third-party adoption evidence. | Customers may prefer established automated tooling. |
| SLSA (https://slsa.dev/) | Supply-chain integrity framework and provenance levels. | QPF can help explain a project's public evidence and verification path. | QPF does not provide SLSA conformance or provenance guarantees. | Customers may need standards compliance rather than narrative/evidence packaging. |
| Sigstore (https://www.sigstore.dev/) | Signing and verification tooling for software artifacts. | QPF can link artifact evidence into a broader transparency package. | QPF does not replace artifact signing or verification infrastructure. | Customers may already meet needs with Sigstore alone. |
| Traditional audit firms | Paid security and compliance assessment. | QPF can prepare evidence and documentation before a formal audit. | QPF is not an audit firm and cannot issue certification or an audit opinion. | Buyers may require recognized audit credentials. |
| Developer-relations or technical-writing agencies | Documentation, onboarding, and community content. | QPF combines documentation with QPF-style public verification practice. | QPF has no completed third-party track record showing this combination. | Buyers may choose established agencies with portfolios. |

## Honest positioning

QPF's near-term position is a bounded evidence-readiness and
deployment-transparency preparation service. It should not claim to replace
security scanners, supply-chain standards, signing infrastructure, or formal
audits.
