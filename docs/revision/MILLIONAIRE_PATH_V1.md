## Offer (sell this, only this)

### 1. Self-serve Claim Check — free
Browser or curl. One address or one repo. Establishes / does not establish / unknown.
Purpose: lead magnet. Not the business.

### 2. Signed Claim Report — CAD $3,500
One bounded claim. 8-page report + machine receipt + reproduction commands.
Delivery: 5 business days.
Refund: if reproduction commands fail on your published evidence, full refund.

### 3. Launch Evidence Pack — CAD $12,000
Up to 5 claims. Deployed addresses, bytecode presence, liquidity state, admin-key posture, “do not claim X” language lock.
Delivery: 10 business days.
This is the product that can pay rent.

### 4. 90-day Watch — CAD $2,000 / month
Re-run the same claims monthly. Alert if bytecode, ownership, or reserves change.
This is how $12k becomes recurring.

Do not sell $250 reports. That price trains people that the work is a hobby.
