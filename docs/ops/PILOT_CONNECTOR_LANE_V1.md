## 2. First mistake to avoid

| Do not | Do |
| --- | --- |
| “I need users / thousands of adopters” | “I need **one** person with a painful problem willing to try” |
| Sell the ecosystem | Ask for a **reality check** |
| Lead with architecture | Lead with **their** workflow |

**Pilot #001** is the unit of progress — not a platform launch.

## 3. Where to look (start here)

### Circle 1 — people who already know you (easiest first user)

| Source | Why easier |
| --- | --- |
| Friends | Trust already exists |
| Former coworkers | Shared context |
| Online contacts | Warm or lukewarm |
| Developer communities you already inhabit | Familiar tone |
| People who have followed the project | Curiosity without cold sales |

**Message frame (not sales):**

> Would you spend 30 minutes trying it on a real problem?

### Circle 2 — only after Circle 1 is moving

Communities where **repetitive work pain** shows up (docs, admin, research overload, private AI curiosity) — still human-selected targets, no scraping blasts.

## 4. Outreach scripts (human paste)

### Script A — warm / knows you (preferred)

```text
Hey — I'm testing a system I've been building and I'm looking for 3 people
to try it on a real workflow. I'm not selling anything — I'm trying to measure
whether it actually saves someone time.

Would you be willing to give me 30 minutes and tell me honestly what works
and what doesn't?
```

### Script B — slightly more specific

```text
Hey — I've spent months on a private AI operator setup (under your control,
no wallet / no crypto risk). I need a reality check from someone outside
the project.

If you have one repetitive task that eats time (docs, admin, research notes,
status chaos), would you do a 30–60 min session with me? Goal is only to
measure time/friction and whether you'd want to do it again.
```

### Script C — short DM

```text
Quick ask: 30 min reality check on something I built — one real workflow,
honest feedback, not a sales pitch. Open this week?
```

**Human send only.** Edit tone to sound like you.

## 5. Week 1 operation (like a drill)

### Objective

```text
10 conversations booked
(not 10 customers)
```

A “conversation” can be: coffee chat, call, or pilot session. Booking is the Week 1 success metric. Closing Pilot #001 is the Week 1–2 stretch goal.

### Success signal (the one that matters)

```text
One person says: "Can we do this again?"
```

### Daily rhythm (example)

| Day | Target |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | List 15 Circle 1 names (private; do not commit PII) |
| 2 | Send 5 Script A/B messages |
| 3 | Send 5 more · follow up 2 non-replies lightly |
| 4 | First conversations / sessions |
| 5–7 | More conversations · first evidence card if a session completed |

**Volume over perfection.** Ten honest “no”s beat zero sends while polishing the product.

## 7. Interviewer mode (30 min conversation)

You are not pitching. You are learning.

| Minute | Do |
| --- | --- |
| 0–5 | Their world: what eats time this week? |
| 5–10 | Before estimate: how long does that usually take? |
| 10–25 | Try one path (E1 / their task) **or** deep interview if they can’t sit a demo yet |
| 25–30 | Would you do this again? What almost made you quit? One quote? |

**Listen for:**

- pain that **repeats weekly**  
- willingness to try again  
- friction that is **docs/UX** (fix) vs **missing fantasy features** (defer)
