# QPF Verification Pilot Invitation Draft - Internal v1

**Status:** Draft only. Do not send, publish, or personalize without explicit
human authorization.

## Recipient selection criteria

Use this draft only for a maintainer who:

1. Has one public technical claim they want inspected.
2. Can provide the public sources needed to examine that claim.
3. Could use a clear record of what the available evidence supports, does not
   support, or leaves unknown.

Do not use it for unsolicited bulk outreach, financial solicitation, or a
claim that QPF can provide a formal security audit, legal conclusion, or
guaranteed outcome.

## Draft message

**Subject:** Optional QPF evidence review pilot for [project or claim]

Hello [name],

I am preparing one small QPF verification pilot for a public technical claim.
The pilot reviews the public evidence you provide and returns a concise,
inspectable summary that distinguishes what is verified, unverified, gated,
or unknown.

If you are interested, the proposed scope is one claim and its relevant
public references. The resulting package records the checks performed,
evidence sources, findings, and limits. It is not a security audit, legal
opinion, investment recommendation, or guarantee.

There is no obligation to participate. If the pilot sounds useful, reply with
the claim you would want examined and the relevant public links. We can then
decide whether the scope is appropriate before any work begins.

Thank you,

[human sender name]

## Required human review before sending

1. Confirm the recipient and project are appropriate.
2. Replace all placeholders with truthful, minimal details.
3. Confirm the proposed claim is public and within the pilot boundary.
4. Approve this exact final message for this recipient.
5. Record that the message was sent only after sending occurs.
