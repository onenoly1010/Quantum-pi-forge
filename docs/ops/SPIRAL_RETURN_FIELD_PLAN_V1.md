## Purpose

This plan defines the operational checkpoints for the Spiral Return mobile node field demonstration.

The demonstration proves that Quantum Pi Forge and OINIO can preserve truth surface, receipt integrity, sovereign state, and economic readiness under real-world mobility, variable connectivity, power constraints, and field conditions.

This plan does not authorize live revenue, wallet movement, signing of live transactions, liquidity actions, deployments, treasury routing, automated fees, or mainnet mutation.

All execution remains local-first, dry-run, simulated, observational, or receipt-only until a future explicit operational gate is created and approved by the human operator.

## Operational Objectives

### 1. Mobile Node Continuity Validation

- Maintain local-first operation regardless of network availability.
- Connect to 0G Aristotle Mainnet only for read-only status checks where signal allows.
- Gracefully degrade during disconnected zones without loss of local evidence.
- Queue evidence receipts locally using deterministic hashes and filesystem-backed records.
- Reconcile only after human review and only through a future explicit reconciliation gate.
- Continue generating, normalizing, classifying, and sealing proof packets without external dependency.

### 2. Field-Validated Value-Flow Readiness

- Generate MOBILE_NODE_ATTESTATION events at selected field checkpoints.
- Trigger dry-run VERIFY_ACTION events as readiness signals.
- Trigger dry-run MEMORY_CHECKPOINT events as continuity signals.
- Preserve human-in-the-loop review capability for all mobile-generated receipts.
- Ensure every field-generated receipt is manually inspectable after return.

### 3. Core Observables

- Connectivity metadata: connection status, latency where available, uptime estimate, disconnection duration.
- Receipt integrity: local generation, canonical hashing, and preservation of proof packets under travel load.
- Sovereignty proofs: snapshots of canonical head, local evidence index hash, and current gate status.
- Power and hardware metrics: battery state, CPU load, thermal warnings where available, storage utilization.
- Attestation events: timestamped and receipt-hashed field observations.
- Geo context: optional, coarse, privacy-safe location labels only. Precise coordinates are not required.
