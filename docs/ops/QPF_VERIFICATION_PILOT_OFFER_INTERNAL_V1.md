# QPF Verification Pilot Offer - Internal v1

**Status:** Internal preparation only
**Purpose:** Define one small, testable human outcome for QPF's existing
evidence and verification capability.
**Not authorization for:** Publication, outreach, customer commitments,
payment collection, wallet activity, signing, minting, liquidity, or other
chain action.

## The pilot hypothesis

A project maintainer with an important technical claim may find it useful to
receive a concise, inspectable evidence package that distinguishes:

- what public evidence supports;
- what the available evidence does not establish; and
- which checks could not be performed.

The pilot tests usefulness, not market demand, adoption, revenue, or the
accuracy of claims that cannot be independently inspected.

## One-sentence offer

QPF prepares an evidence-backed verification summary of a bounded public
technical claim, clearly labeling verified, unverified, gated, and unknown
findings.

## Intended first participant

One maintainer of a public software, AI-agent, or blockchain project who can
provide a specific claim and the public references needed to inspect it.

This is a selection criterion, not an invitation list or authorization to
contact a person.

## Pilot input

Before any work begins, the participant would supply:

1. A single technical claim to examine.
2. Public source, deployment, or documentation references relevant to it.
3. The intended audience for the resulting summary.
4. Permission to retain or publish any resulting artifact, if requested.

If the claim, scope, or evidence source is ambiguous, the pilot must be
paused rather than inferred.

## Pilot deliverable

The proposed output is one reviewable evidence package containing:

| Section | Required content |
| --- | --- |
| Claim | The participant's exact claim and scope |
| Method | Reproducible checks and evidence sources used |
| Findings | Explicit `verified`, `unverified`, `gated`, or `unknown` labels |
| Limits | Checks not performed and conclusions not supported |
| Evidence index | Links, revisions, hashes, or other stable references |
| Participant response | Actual usefulness, confusion, or unmet need |

The package is not a security audit, legal opinion, investment recommendation,
guarantee, or statement about future outcomes.

## Success evidence

The pilot is informative if it captures an actual participant response to the
package, including a neutral or negative outcome. Useful evidence may show
that the package:

- clarified whether a claim was supported;
- exposed documentation or deployment drift;
- reduced time needed to inspect an issue; or
- failed to solve the participant's problem.

No response, payment, adoption, or testimonial may be assumed.

## Decision points requiring human authorization

The following are separate decisions and are outside this document's scope:

1. Publishing this offer or any related public page.
2. Contacting or inviting a prospective participant.
3. Accepting a participant's claim or committing to deliver work.
4. Setting a price, issuing an invoice, accepting payment, or making a
   financial commitment.
5. Publishing a participant's materials or result.

## Pilot close-out

After a real, authorized interaction, record only observed facts:

1. The claim and scope attempted.
2. Evidence sources and checks performed.
3. The resulting labels and limitations.
4. The participant's response, if provided.
5. The next decision: repeat, revise, or stop.

This record may become a `FIRST_EXTERNAL_ATTEMPT_V1` receipt only after the
event occurs and its evidence is available.
