| Track | Conversations / week | Not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pi ecosystem | **5** | Sales pitches |
| 0G ecosystem | **5** | Architecture dumps |
| AI developers | **5** | “Join our ecosystem” ads |
| **Total** | **15 discovery touches** | Feature factory week |

A “conversation” can be: DM reply thread, voice call, Discord reply, or short session. Quality > polished pitch.

### Discovery questions (use these)

1. What are you building?  
2. What’s your biggest workflow pain?  
3. Would this help?  
4. If not, what would?  

If **2–3 conversations** surface the **same pain**, that is a stronger product signal than another AI review cycle.

### 15-minute answer (refine and reuse)

**Core:**

```text
In 15 minutes you can run one real workflow with a private AI operator
(under your control) and leave with a clear answer: did this save you time
on a task you already have — or not? No wallet. No pitch. Honest result.
```

**For Pi builders:**

```text
In 15 minutes: one concrete task (docs, verify, understand a flow) with a
private AI helper aimed at Pi/0G builders — so you can decide if it solves
a problem you already have, not watch a product tour.
```

**For 0G / AI-infra builders:**

```text
In 15 minutes: try one workflow (verify, summarize, automate a step) with a
prepare-only AI operator + evidence-style reports — feedback on fit, not a sell.
```

**For local AI / OSS (Ollama, Aider, Continue, MCP):**

```text
In 15 minutes: see how a multi-agent cockpit + shared verify reports sits
next to your local stack — authority stays human; AI prepares only.
```

## Pi approach (builders inside the large community)

Large community ≠ automatic users. Find **builders**.

**Do not say:** “Here’s my project.”

**Say:**

```text
I built an open-source AI infrastructure that integrates with 0G and is
designed to support the Pi ecosystem. I'd like feedback from Pi developers
on whether this solves problems you actually have.
```

That invites **discussion**, not promotion.
